Our Roots & Heritage

Liberty For Everyone

"We have on this earth what makes life worth living." Born from heritage, resilience, and an unwavering commitment to bringing people together through beverage.

A Family Endeavor

Rooted in Safad.
Founded in Malmö.

With a vision of a united society, our goal is clear: to never give up in our pursuit of community, dignity, and shared understanding.

Safad Food AB was established by a Palestinian family residing in Malmö, Sweden. We produce and distribute world-class sparkling beverages that celebrate our cultural roots while opening a global conversation.

Our cans are not merely soft drinks. They carry a timeless statement of identity, freedom, and peaceful resilience: "Never stop talking about Palestine." Every batch is bottled with premium natural water, balanced carbonation, and carefully formulated sweetness.
