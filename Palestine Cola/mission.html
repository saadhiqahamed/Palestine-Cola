Our Manifesto

More Than A Soda.
A Principled Stand.

Safad Food AB is more than a commercial company. As a family-owned enterprise with deep Palestinian roots, we aim to produce exceptionally high-quality carbonated beverages while creating a sustainable pathway of support for communities that need it most.

Partner Organization

Safad Foundation

Palestine Drinks proudly operates in partnership with the Safad Foundation — an autonomous charitable non-profit organization dedicated to humanitarian relief, education, and sustainable community empowerment in Palestine.

Safad Food AB (the Swedish commercial entity) and Safad Foundation (the humanitarian organization) operate under separate governance structures to guarantee full regulatory transparency, fiscal integrity, and ethical independence.
