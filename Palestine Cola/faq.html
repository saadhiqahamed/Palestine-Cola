Palestine Cola is a refreshing cola drink crafted by Safad Food AB, a Palestinian family-owned company based in Malmö, Sweden. It perfectly balances bubbles and sweetness, offering a moment of joy and freedom with every sip. More than just a beverage, Palestine Cola is a statement of solidarity — a way to never stop talking about Palestine.
Palestine Drinks currently offers Palestine Cola, Palestine Cola Sugarfree, and Palestine Energy Drink, all available now. Palestine Orange and Palestine Lemon Lime are launching soon.
Palestine Cola Sugarfree brings you the iconic taste of Palestine Cola without the sugar. It features the same fresh character and perfectly balanced fizz — ideal for those seeking a sugar-free drink with full flavor. Skip the sugar, keep the freedom.
Palestine Energy is an energy drink designed to deliver a refreshing burst of flavor while keeping you focused and energized throughout the day. It contains Vitamin B3, Vitamin B6, and Vitamin B12. Whether you need energy for your workday, a workout, or daily challenges — Palestine Energy has you covered.

Palestine Drinks was created with a clear purpose: to deliver high-quality beverages while supporting Palestinian communities. Every purchase contributes to the empowerment and sustainability of Palestinian communities. The brand's core message is 'Never stop talking about Palestine.'
The Safad Foundation is an independent charitable organization that shares the same vision as Palestine Drinks but operates separately from Safad Food AB, the commercial company. The foundation focuses on humanitarian work and community empowerment for Palestinians. You can learn more at safadfoundation.com ↗.
You can reach Palestine Drinks through our contact form on the Contact page, by phone at +46 (0)762 457 724, or by visiting us at Emilstorpsgatan 23, Malmö, Sweden. Safad Food AB, Org. no. 5593437741.
Palestine Drinks and Safad Food AB are not affiliated with any political party or political organization. The brand is a commercial beverage company with Palestinian roots, focused on culture, community, and humanitarian support through commerce.
