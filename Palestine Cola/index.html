Our Heritage

Liberty For
Everyone

With a vision of a united society, our goal is clear: to never give up in our pursuit of community, dignity, and shared understanding.

Safad Food AB is a Palestinian family-run enterprise based in Malmö, Sweden. We formulate and bottle carbonated beverages crafted to Scandinavian quality standards — carrying cultural pride and purpose with every sip.

When you drink Palestine Drinks, you're not just enjoying a beverage — you're standing with us in our core mission: "Never stop talking about Palestine."

Safad Food AB is more than a beverage company. As a family-owned enterprise with deep Palestinian roots, we aim to produce high-quality beverages while generating tangible support for communities in need.

Partner Organization

Safad Foundation

Palestine Drinks operates alongside the Safad Foundation — an independent charitable organization dedicated to supporting Palestinian communities. While Safad Food AB is the commercial company behind Palestine Drinks, the Safad Foundation is an autonomous charitable entity focused on humanitarian work and community empowerment.
